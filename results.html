Instructor setup: connect this page to your Google Sheet

This dashboard reads your responses straight from the same Sheet the survey writes to. One-time setup:

  1. Open the Google Sheet the survey feeds into.
  2. Go to File → Share → Publish to web.
  3. Under "Link", choose the specific sheet/tab with your responses, set the format dropdown to Comma-separated values (.csv), then click Publish.
  4. Copy the URL it gives you.
  5. In this file, find const CSV_URL = "PASTE_YOUR_PUBLISHED_CSV_URL_HERE"; near the bottom and paste it in.

The dashboard re-reads that URL on every page load, so it always reflects the latest submissions. It expects the same column names the survey form sends: Course, Term, Attendance, Pace, Clarity, Helpful_Lectures, Helpful_Labs, Helpful_ProblemSets, Helpful_Discussion, Helpful_OfficeHours, Workload, What_Worked, What_To_Change, Recommend, Other_Comments.
